FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Email us at [YOUR EMAIL].

Is Wayzo free?+

Yes, it's free during early access. Premium extras come later, and core trip planning stays free.

Do I need to install an app?+

No. Wayzo runs in your browser on laptop and phone, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

Which countries does it work in?+

Wayzo is built to work wherever you travel: city breaks, beach weeks or multi-country trips.

Does Wayzo book things for me?+

It suggests and links you to trusted booking partners. Once you book, forward the confirmation and it's added automatically.

Is my ticket data safe?+

Trips are private by default and stored encrypted. You can delete a trip and its data anytime.

Does it work with WhatsApp?+

Not yet. WhatsApp is coming soon, so you'll be able to forward tickets and get your brief in chat. Waitlist members get it first.
